For applicants planning to work, study, relocate with their families or complete overseas business formalities, understanding these differences before submitting documents can help prevent avoidable delays and repeated visits to different authorities.

A degree certificate intended for employment in the United Arab Emirates may follow a different authentication route from a birth certificate required for a family visa or a marriage certificate submitted to a foreign civil registration authority. ## Apostille or Embassy Attestation: Which Route Is Relevant?

Apostille and normal attestation are different authentication routes. Choosing between them at the beginning can help you avoid arranging an unsuitable service.

### Apostille for eligible destination countries

India is a member of the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention. An apostille is used for eligible public documents intended for use in countries where the Convention applies. The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) explains its apostille and normal attestation procedures on its [official attestation and apostille page](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm).

Depending on the document and applicable procedure, authentication by the relevant designated authority may be required before the document can be submitted for apostille.

### Normal attestation and embassy legalisation

For destinations where apostille is not accepted and normal attestation is required, the document may need authentication by the relevant Indian authorities followed by the applicable embassy or consular legalisation process.

The exact sequence depends on the document, destination and current requirements of the authorities involved. Some countries or receiving organisations may also prescribe additional steps.

**Important:** Do not select apostille or embassy attestation solely on the basis of a country name mentioned informally. Confirm the current requirement with the organisation that will receive your document and check the applicable official guidance.

## Certificate Attestation in Delhi for Different Document Categories

The authentication route is influenced by the type of document being submitted. Identifying the category correctly helps determine which preliminary checks may be necessary.

### 1. Educational certificate attestation

Educational documents commonly include:

- Degree and postgraduate degree certificates
- Diploma certificates
- School certificates and mark sheets
- Academic transcripts
- Other certificates issued by recognised educational institutions

For educational documents, the issuing university, board, institution and applicable state-level verification requirements can affect the procedure. A document issued in one state should not automatically be assumed to follow the same preliminary authentication route as a document issued in another.

Before submission, check that the certificate details are clear and consistent with the applicant's supporting identification. If an employer or university has specified a particular document, such as a degree certificate rather than a provisional certificate, confirm that distinction before proceeding.

### 2. Personal document attestation

Personal documents may include birth certificates, marriage certificates, affidavits, powers of attorney and other civil or personal records.

The required authentication can depend on the document's issuing authority and intended purpose. For example, a birth certificate required for a dependent visa may have different supporting-document requirements from a marriage certificate requested for overseas registration.

Check whether the receiving authority requires an original civil certificate, a particular version of the record or an approved translation. A notarised copy should not automatically be treated as a substitute for the original document when the authority specifically requires the original.

### 3. Employment and other supporting documents

Some overseas employment, immigration and administrative applications require employment certificates, experience letters or additional supporting records.

Before arranging attestation, confirm whether the receiving authority requires authentication of the document itself, verification by the issuing employer, a particular declaration or another form of supporting evidence. The appropriate procedure should be established from the actual requirement rather than assumed from the document title alone.

## Why the Issuing State and Authority Can Change the Process

Delhi is a major centre for document-related services, but the location where you arrange assistance does not necessarily determine which authority must authenticate your certificate first.

For example, an educational certificate issued by a university in one state may need preliminary verification through the applicable authority associated with that institution or state. A personal document issued by a state authority may follow a different authentication route.

The MEA's published guidance describes preliminary authentication by designated authorities and the subsequent process for eligible documents. Requirements may vary according to document category and the applicable route.

This is why applicants seeking certificate attestation in Delhi should establish the following before submitting their documents:

1. **Place of issuance:** Identify the state, board, university, department or authority that issued the document.
2. **Document category:** Establish whether it is educational, personal, commercial or another type of record.
3. **Pre-authentication requirement:** Confirm which authority, if any, must verify the document before MEA processing.
4. **Destination requirement:** Establish whether apostille or normal attestation is applicable.
5. **Additional formalities:** Check for translations, supporting identification, certified copies or embassy-specific requirements.

Following the correct sequence is more important than simply submitting the document to the nearest office.

## Common Mistakes That Can Delay Certificate Attestation

### Choosing the process before confirming the destination

Arranging the wrong form of authentication may require additional steps. Establish the receiving country's requirements first.

### Assuming every certificate follows the same route

A degree certificate, birth certificate and power of attorney are different document types. Their issuing authorities and preliminary authentication requirements may differ.

### Ignoring the issuing authority

A document's place of issuance can affect which authority is competent to authenticate it. Confirm the appropriate route rather than relying on a general process description.

### Submitting incomplete or unclear paperwork

Missing supporting records, inconsistent personal details or an unsuitable document format may lead to further verification or resubmission.

### Relying only on an estimated completion date

Processing time can depend on preliminary verification, the issuing institution, the relevant government authority, the destination country's requirements and the workload of the authorities involved. Request a case-specific estimate rather than assuming that every application can be completed within the same period.

### Confusing document authentication with acceptance

Attestation or apostille authenticates the relevant signature, seal or capacity within the applicable system. It does not necessarily establish that the contents of the document are true or guarantee that a foreign employer, university or government authority will accept it for every purpose. The receiving organisation's own eligibility and document rules still apply.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What determines the process for certificate attestation in Delhi?

The process depends on the certificate category, issuing authority, destination country and intended use. Preliminary authentication may be required before MEA apostille or normal attestation, and additional legalisation may apply depending on the destination.

### 2. Is apostille the same as embassy attestation?

No. Apostille is used for eligible documents intended for use in countries where the Hague Apostille Convention applies. Normal attestation and embassy legalisation are used where apostille is not accepted or where the applicable requirements call for those steps.

### 3. Can a certificate issued outside Delhi be processed through Delhi?

The location from which you seek assistance does not automatically determine the competent authority for preliminary authentication. The issuing state, institution and applicable procedure must be checked. The available submission route should be confirmed for the specific document.

### 4. How long does certificate attestation take in Delhi?

There is no single reliable timeline for every document. The time required can depend on preliminary verification, the issuing authority, the authentication route and any embassy or consular formalities. Request an estimate after the document and destination requirements have been assessed.

### 5. Are original certificates required for attestation?

Requirements vary by document and procedure. Some routes require original documents, while certain processes may accept specified forms of certified documentation. Confirm the current official requirements before submitting any original certificate.

### 6. Does an apostille guarantee that a foreign organisation will accept my certificate?

No. Apostille addresses authentication under the applicable Convention framework; it does not guarantee that a receiving organisation will accept the document for a particular application. The organisation may have separate rules regarding eligibility, translations, document format or supporting evidence.

### 7. What should I provide when enquiring about certificate attestation?

Start with the document type, issuing authority or state, destination country and purpose. Explain any instructions received from the employer, university or government department. These details help determine which requirements need to be verified before the process begins.
